EzStuff privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026.

EzStuff is a personal tool with one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else. This page says what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

EzStuff asks Google for one permission, drive.file. With it, EzStuff can see and change only the files it created itself in its owner's Google Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file, and it reads no other Google data: no email, contacts, calendar or profile.

What it does with that access

Once a day it uploads a new version of its own file, a SQLite database of public quiz-game data, and deletes that file's older versions so they do not use the owner's storage. It does nothing else in Google Drive.

What it collects and shares

Nothing. EzStuff collects no personal data, sends no Google user data to anyone, and shows no advertising. The file it uploads holds only data already published on gotquestions.online and rating.chgk.info.

Where the sign-in is kept

The sign-in Google issues is stored on the owner's own server and is used only by EzStuff, to upload its file.

How to remove its access

Open your Google Account, go to Security, then "Your connections to third-party apps & services", choose EzStuff, and remove its access. EzStuff then cannot reach Google Drive at all.
